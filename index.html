Ready. The first run downloads 122 MiB of weights; after that the models stay resident.

Audio is 22,050 Hz mono 16-bit PCM. Sentences are separated by 200 ms of silence. Sentences longer than 768 tokens are split at a word boundary, and a part whose predicted duration exceeds the model's 1024-frame limit is halved and retried until it fits, with 200 ms of silence standing in for anything that still does not. The single-sentence page is at single_sentence.html.

See the Github repository for links to the original Matcha-TTS and HiFi vocoder projects (both MIT licensed).
