Matcha-TTS and HiFi vocoder port to WebGPU

Text is phonemized with eSpeak NG, tokenized exactly as Matcha-TTS does, then run through the hand-translated WebGPU kernels — acoustic model and HiFi vocoder — to produce speech.

Limit: 768 tokens (about 383 phonemes). Press Ctrl/Cmd + Enter to run.

Ready.

Audio is 22,050 Hz mono 16-bit PCM, generated entirely on the GPU. The mel export uses the matcha-tts-mel-v1 format, so run_tts_pipeline.py --matcha-json matcha_mel.json can reproduce the same WAV on CUDA.
